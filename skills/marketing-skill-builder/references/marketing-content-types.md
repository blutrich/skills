# Marketing Content Types

This reference covers common marketing content types. Use it when mapping content needs for a marketing skill.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn

**Constraints:**
- 3,000 character limit (but 150-300 chars show before "see more")
- Algorithm favors engagement in first hour
- Native content outperforms links

**Format patterns that work:**

Hook + story + insight + CTA:
```
[1-2 sentence hook that stops scrolling]

[3-5 sentences telling a specific story]

[2-3 sentences extracting the lesson]

[Question or soft CTA]
```

List posts:
```
[Bold claim about the topic]

Here's what [success/failure] actually looks like:

→ Point 1
→ Point 2
→ Point 3
→ Point 4
→ Point 5

[Closing thought or question]
```

**Avoid:**
- Starting with "I'm excited to announce..."
- Hashtag stuffing
- Asking for engagement explicitly ("Like if you agree!")

### Twitter/X

**Constraints:**
- 280 characters per tweet
- Threads can go longer
- Quote tweets often outperform replies

**Format patterns:**

Single tweet:
```
[Observation or hot take] + [Supporting point or twist]
```

Thread opener:
```
[Big claim or question]

Here's what I learned: 🧵
```

Thread format:
```
1/ [Hook]
2/ [Context/Background]
3-7/ [Main points]
8/ [Conclusion or CTA]
```

**Avoid:**
- Ending tweets with "Thoughts?"
- Excessive emoji
- More than 3 hashtags

### Instagram

**Constraints:**
- 2,200 character caption limit
- First 125 characters show before "more"
- Hashtags in comments vs. caption (test both)

**Caption format:**

Short (engagement focused):
```
[Punchy statement or question]

[1-2 sentences of context]

[CTA: Tag someone, save this, comment]
```

Long (value focused):
```
[Hook line]

[3-5 paragraphs of value]

[CTA]

.
.
.
[Hashtags]
```

**Avoid:**
- Walls of text with no line breaks
- Generic CTAs ("Link in bio!")
- Hashtags that don't match content

## Email Marketing

### Newsletter

**Constraints:**
- Subject line: 40-60 characters ideal
- Preview text: 40-130 characters
- Mobile-first design

**Subject line patterns:**

Question: "Is your [thing] actually working?"
Number: "3 mistakes killing your [outcome]"
Curiosity gap: "The [topic] trick nobody talks about"
Direct: "[Topic]: Here's what changed"

**Body structure:**

```
[Personal opener: 1-2 sentences]

[Main content: 150-400 words]

[Key takeaway or CTA]

[Sign-off]

[P.S. with secondary CTA or teaser]
```

**Avoid:**
- Starting with "I hope this email finds you well"
- Burying the point below the fold
- Multiple competing CTAs

### Cold Email

**Constraints:**
- Subject: 1-5 words works best
- Body: Under 150 words
- One clear ask

**Format:**

```
[1 sentence showing you did research]

[1-2 sentences on the problem you solve]

[1 sentence on results you've gotten]

[1 sentence CTA with specific ask]
```

**Avoid:**
- "I came across your profile..."
- "I'd love to pick your brain..."
- Long paragraphs
- Multiple links

### Sales Sequence

**Email 1 (Introduction):**
```
Subject: [Specific reference to their situation]

[Research-backed opener]
[Problem statement]
[Brief intro to solution]
[Soft CTA: "Worth a conversation?"]
```

**Email 2 (Value add):**
```
Subject: [Related to Email 1]

[Quick callback]
[Share valuable insight or resource]
[No direct ask]
```

**Email 3 (Case study):**
```
Subject: How [similar company] did [result]

[Brief story of similar customer]
[Specific outcome with numbers]
[Connect to their situation]
[CTA]
```

**Email 4 (Breakup):**
```
Subject: Should I close your file?

[Acknowledge silence]
[Final value statement]
[Clear CTA or goodbye]
```

## Blog Content

### How-to Posts

**Structure:**

```
# [How to X in Y steps/time]

[Introduction: What they'll learn, why it matters - 2-3 sentences]

## What you'll need / Prerequisites
[Quick list if applicable]

## Step 1: [Action verb + specific outcome]
[2-4 paragraphs of instruction]
[Screenshot or example if helpful]

## Step 2-N: [Same pattern]

## Common mistakes to avoid
[2-3 pitfalls with fixes]

## Next steps
[What to do after completing this]
```

### Listicles

**Structure:**

```
# N [Things/Ways/Reasons] to [Outcome]

[Intro: Why this list matters - 2-3 sentences]

## 1. [Item with specific angle]
[2-3 paragraphs explaining]
[Example or proof point]

## 2-N. [Same pattern]

[Closing: Summary and CTA]
```

### Thought Leadership

**Structure:**

```
# [Provocative claim or question]

[Hook that challenges conventional wisdom]

## The conventional approach
[What most people do/believe]

## Why it's wrong
[Evidence, examples, experience]

## The better way
[Your thesis with supporting points]

## What this means for you
[Practical application]

[Strong closing with call to action or question]
```

## Ad Copy

### Facebook/Instagram Ads

**Primary text patterns:**

Problem-agitate-solve:
```
[State the problem they feel]
[Make it worse/more urgent]
[Introduce the solution]
[CTA]
```

Testimonial lead:
```
"[Quote from customer with specific result]"

[Bridge to offer]
[CTA]
```

**Headline:** 40 characters max, benefit-focused
**Description:** 30 characters max, urgency or specificity

### Google Ads

**Headline 1:** Brand or product name + benefit
**Headline 2:** Feature or offer
**Headline 3:** CTA or trust signal
**Description:** Expand on benefit, include keywords naturally

### LinkedIn Ads

**Format patterns:**

Single image:
```
[Hook question or statement]

[2-3 sentences on the offer]

[CTA: Download, Learn more, Register]
```

## Product Launch

### Announcement

**Structure:**

```
[Hook: What's new in one sentence]

[Why it matters: 2-3 sentences on the problem it solves]

[What it does: 3-5 bullet points of features/benefits]

[Proof: Beta results, testimonials, or demos]

[CTA: How to get it]

[Timeline or urgency if applicable]
```

### Teaser Sequence

**Email 1 (7 days out):**
```
Subject: Something's coming...

[Hint at the problem you're solving]
[Date tease]
[No details]
```

**Email 2 (3 days out):**
```
Subject: [Benefit] is almost here

[More specific hint]
[One feature reveal]
[Save the date]
```

**Email 3 (Launch day):**
```
Subject: It's here: [Product name]

[Full announcement]
[All details]
[Launch offer if applicable]
[CTA]
```

## When Building Content Type Examples

For each content type in a marketing skill, include:

1. **Platform constraints** (character limits, format requirements)
2. **Proven structure** (specific template to follow)
3. **What to avoid** (common mistakes for this type)
4. **2-3 examples** showing input → output transformation

The examples matter most. Show the quality bar clearly.
